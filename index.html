About Me

I am a molecular and human genetics researcher with experience in PCR-based molecular genetics, clinical Sanger chromatogram analysis, whole-exome sequencing variant interpretation, and R/Linux-based transcriptomic workflows.


I contributed to a published sex-stratified RNA-seq study of idiopathic pulmonary arterial hypertension through count-data preparation, downstream analysis, visualization, biological interpretation, and manuscript writing. I also prepared and tested a reproducible CIBERSORTx workflow for disease- and sex-stratified immune-cell infiltration analysis.


My ongoing work includes single-cell and TCGA-based analysis in triple-negative breast cancer, alongside training in clinical and prenatal genetics workflows. My broader interests include rare-disease genomics, neurogenetics, RNA biology, epigenetics, reproductive genetics, and functional genomics.

Selected Projects

Published research · Reproducible R workflow

IPAH Transcriptomics & Immune-Cell Infiltration

Sex-stratified transcriptomic analysis of idiopathic pulmonary arterial hypertension. Contributions included RNA-seq count-data preparation, downstream analysis and visualization in R, biological interpretation, and manuscript writing.

The accompanying repository provides a tested workflow for TPM preparation, CIBERSORTx LM22 processing, disease- and sex-stratified comparisons, Benjamini–Hochberg correction, Cliff's delta effect-size analysis, and reproducible figure generation.
